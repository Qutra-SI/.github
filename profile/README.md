<p align="center"><b>A local-first personal AI assistant for the desktop.</b></p>

Qutra lives on your computer. It knows your preferences, looks at your screen when you ask,
listens, talks back, keeps your notes and tasks — and acts on your behalf only with your permission.

### Principles

- **Local and private by default** — your data stays on your device; anything sent to the cloud is visible and configurable.
- **Permission first** — every risky action goes through an explicit, native consent step.
- **Turkish first** — interface, voice and model choices are tuned for Turkish, with English as the second language.
- **Open standards** — `SKILL.md` skills, MCP plugins, plain Markdown notes, GGUF models.

### Status

In active development — Phase 0, foundations. Windows first, then macOS and Linux.
The source code is private for now.
